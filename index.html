Convert spreadsheet data into rich text for your content management system (CMS)


      
Prepare your CSV file

      
    
        
  • Keep only the data you need in your spreadsheet (no formulas—just plain text).
    • 
        
  • Make sure your column headers are clearly labeled (this matters later).
    • 
        
  • Export or save the file as a CSV.
    • 
      


      
Generate preview in MarinMagic

      
    
        
  • Open MarinMagic and upload your CSV file.
    • 
      

      
In Field Mapping:

      
    
        
  1. Choose a heading column (this will be the title for each section).
    2. 
        
  2. Select the heading level under “Top-level.”
    3. 
        
  3. Choose the columns to include in your list (don’t include the heading column).
    4. 
        
  4. Drag to reorder items if needed.
    5. 
      

      
    
        
  • Click Generate preview
    • 
      


      
Update your page in the CMS

      
    
        
  • Open or create the page in your CMS and click Edit.
    • 
      

      
Go to the Sections tab:

      
    
        
  1. Create or edit existing Text section.
    2. 
        
  2. If replacing content, select all existing text and delete it.
    3. 
      

      
In MarinMagic:

      
    
        
  1. Copy the generated rich text from the preview.
    2. 
      

      
    
        
  • In your CMS, paste it into the Content field on the page.
    • 
        
  • Remove the anchor links filter at the top (not needed yet).
    • 
        
  • Click Preview to review your page.
    • 
        
  • When ready, set status to Published and Save.
