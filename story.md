# Story: MarinMagic

## Overview

Through a collaborative “Innovation Hour” working session, County staff rapidly developed and deployed a lightweight solution that improves transparency for Environmental Health Services (EHS) applicants while reducing administrative burden on staff.

The new workflow allows food establishments to instantly access their Plan Review submission status online instead of calling County offices for updates. Developed in just a few days through agile collaboration and AI-assisted development, the solution demonstrates how small cross-functional teams can quickly deliver meaningful operational improvements.

## The Challenge

Previously, food establishments had limited visibility into the status of their Plan Review submissions. Applicants frequently contacted Board of Supervisors aides and Community Development Agency (CDA) staff for updates, creating unnecessary interruptions and increasing staff workload.

At the same time, staff faced challenges converting spreadsheet-based operational data into web-ready content quickly enough to keep information current and accessible.

## The Approach

The solution emerged from “Innovation Hour,” a weekly informal collaboration session where staff bring real operational pain points and work together to prototype solutions in real time.

After identifying the spreadsheet-to-web publishing challenge, staff rapidly co-developed a lightweight tool called MarinMagic that converts spreadsheet data into Drupal-ready content.

Within approximately 24 hours:
- A working prototype was developed
- EHS staff provided immediate feedback
- The workflow was refined and aligned with operational needs
- A live Plan Review status page was launched

This rapid iteration model enabled immediate deployment while incorporating direct business-user input throughout the process.

## The Solution

The resulting solution provides a live online status page for EHS Plan Review submissions.

Key features include:
- Spreadsheet-to-web content automation
- Self-service status visibility for applicants
- Faster content updates by staff
- Drupal-ready publishing workflow
- Alignment with existing intake and operational processes

The solution is now being incorporated into daily operations, including directing applicants to the online status page during intake.

## Results & Impact

The project delivered immediate operational benefits:

- Reduced phone calls and emails to staff
- Improved transparency for applicants
- Faster publishing and update workflows
- Enabled staff to update content independently in minutes
- Reduced interruptions to BOS aides and CDA staff
- Demonstrated practical use of agile collaboration and AI-assisted development

The success of the project has already generated interest in identifying additional use cases across divisions.
